01 — ABOUT ME

More than a developer. A builder of people & technology.

I’m Rafiul Islam, a Full-Stack Developer passionate about SaaS, technology, and product growth. Beyond coding, I enjoy connecting with people, building communities, leading initiatives, and turning ideas into action.
